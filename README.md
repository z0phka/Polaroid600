# Polaroid600

![Polaroid Now, Polaroid Go, Polaroid 635, Polaroid Spectra](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/11a0f3ee3f9008b502b3712c1b766b3b4f42c7b6.png)

Polaroid600 is a mod that adds various Polaroid instant cameras and film types.

## Features
### Cameras
The mod features 5 different cameras with different features and film types, including the 80s Polaroid 635, the (now sadly dead) Polaroid Spectra and the modern Polaroid Now.

### Film

![Color, Black and White, Blue, Green and Purple Film items](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/1b54b0459def16aff0347d6b5a3d82f0995750ad.png)

In addition to the Color film, the mod offers the Black & White, Blue, Green and Purple films, based on real-life Polaroid film selection.

### Effects

|![Polaroid photo without effects](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/584a794963a07912ef1771059c9d98baad265286.png)|![Polaroid photo with effects](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/46514ae7899fc6be95c38342bcfa86346cbd5f33.png)|
| -------- | ------- |

Various image processing effects are applied to the captured photos, based on the camera, its state and loaded film, including depth-of-field, chromatic aberration, or double exposures.

### Polaroid Walls

![Polaroid photos wall](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/1c78a0b055046b4e1db7b7006d46c0a51b2cd283.png)

You can place your developed photos on a wall in a grid for others to see.

## Compatibility
The mod is generally compatible with most mods. However, in the case of mods that heavily affect rendering - like Sodium or Iris - the compatibility may be restricted (for example, some features or effects might not fully work). For more information on compatibility, see the wiki.
